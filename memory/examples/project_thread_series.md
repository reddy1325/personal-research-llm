Long-form thread series on a single topic, published in order over several weeks. Each part stands alone but builds on the prior.

**Why:** The series format compounds reader retention; each post pulls newcomers into the prior arc. Single posts on the same material would underperform.

**How to apply:** Maintain the series order in this memory. Default to source-faithful exposition for the body of the series; reserve defensive or polemical framings for the explicit closing post. When drafting a new part, re-read the prior part to maintain continuity of voice and reference frame.
