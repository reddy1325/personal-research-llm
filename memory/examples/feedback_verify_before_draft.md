---
name: feedback-verify-before-draft
description: Verify load-bearing facts before writing the draft, not after
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

When a draft hinges on a specific fact (a number, a date, an attribution, a translation), verify it before drafting. Annotate the draft with a verification line so the source is traceable.

**Why:** Fact errors caught after drafting create rework and risk shipping a wrong claim if review is rushed. Verifying first means the draft is built on a sound foundation rather than fixed retroactively.

**How to apply:** Identify the load-bearing claim. Web-search or check the wiki. Quote the source verbatim in the draft notes. If the fact cannot be verified to confidence, either drop the claim or flag it as contested.
